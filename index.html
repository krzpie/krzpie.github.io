Principal investigator

Krzysztof Pietrzak

Professor of Computer Science

Krzysztof Pietrzak works on foundational and practical questions in cryptography. His research includes proofs of space and time, memory-hard functions, leakage-resilient cryptography, and secure decentralized systems. He joined ISTA in 2011.

Research

Security, from first principles.

We connect rigorous security theory with systems that face practical constraints and adversaries.

01

Sustainable blockchains

Proofs of space, verifiable delay functions, and consensus protocols that avoid the resource cost of proof of work.

02

Secure group messaging

Protocols that scale private, authenticated communication to large and changing groups.

03

Leakage-resilient cryptography

Schemes designed to remain secure when physical implementations leak information or are tampered with.

04

Cryptographic complexity

Memory-hard functions, proof systems, and the assumptions and reductions underlying provable security.
